// Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct operational answers before pipeline deployment.

Will outreach affect our main company domain?
Outreach is run through dedicated secondary sending domains and mailboxes, keeping your primary corporate domain completely separated from campaign activity.
How do you verify lead quality?
We research accounts against your agreed ICP, identify relevant decision-makers, and verify business contact details before adding them to any outreach sequence.
How long does pipeline setup take?
Infrastructure provisioning, DNS authentication (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), and initial mailbox warm-up require a standard ramp-up period before outreach goes fully live.
What happens after a prospect shows interest?
Nexora monitors responses, handles initial qualification, captures conversation context, and routes the opportunity directly to your sales team for the call.
How does pricing work?
Engagements are structured around setup, agreed pipeline deliverables, and performance parameters depending on your sales targets and target vertical.
